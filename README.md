# India CPI Inflation Tracker | 2013-2023

## Overview
Interactive Excel dashboard analyzing 10 years of India's Consumer Price Index 
data across 27 categories, built with Power Query and Pivot Tables.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Power Query (data cleaning & transformation)
- Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts
- Conditional Formatting (heatmap)

## Key Findings
- General CPI rose from 110 (2013) to 177.6 (2023)
- Oils & Fats hit peak avg of 192.3 in 2022 (Ukraine war impact)
- Fuel & Light accelerated post-COVID reaching 182 in 2023
- Rural inflation consistently 2.5 pts higher than Urban

## Dataset
Source: All India CPI Index (Ministry of Statistics, Govt. of India
