🇮🇳 Decoding India's Inflation: CPI Analytics Dashboard

A comprehensive Excel-based Data Analytics project focused on understanding inflation trends in India using Consumer Price Index (CPI) data from 2013–2023.

This project analyzes key drivers of inflation, category-wise contribution to CPI, food inflation trends, COVID-19 impact, and the relationship between energy prices and inflation categories.

📌 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to derive actionable insights from India's CPI dataset and answer key business and policy-oriented questions:

Which categories contribute the most to CPI?
How has inflation evolved over time?
What drove food inflation during 2022–2023?
How did COVID-19 affect inflation patterns?
Which categories are most sensitive to energy price fluctuations?
📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard contains six analytical sections:

1. CPI Basket Contribution Analysis

Identifies the contribution of broader categories toward the overall CPI basket.

Key Finding:

Food emerged as the largest contributor to India's CPI basket.
2. YoY Inflation Trend Analysis (2013–2023)

Analyzes year-over-year inflation growth using the General CPI Index.

Key Finding:

Inflation growth peaked in 2021 following post-pandemic economic recovery and supply chain disruptions.
3. Food Inflation Deep Dive

Examines food inflation for the 12 months ending May 2023.

Analysis Includes:

Month-on-month food inflation trend
Highest and lowest inflation months
Absolute change in food inflation
Top contributing food categories

Key Findings:

Highest Food Inflation: October 2022 (1.01%)
Lowest Food Inflation: December 2022 (-1.35%)
Top Contributor: Spices (+30.9 index points)
4. COVID-19 Impact Analysis

Compares inflation trends before and after the onset of COVID-19.

Categories Analyzed:

General CPI
Food
Healthcare
Household Goods & Services

Key Finding:

Healthcare showed the highest impact, increasing by 60.02 index points during the post-COVID period.
5. Energy Price Impact Analysis

Investigates the relationship between energy prices and inflation categories using correlation analysis.

Note:
Imported crude oil price data was unavailable; therefore, the Fuel & Light index was used as a proxy for energy prices.

Key Findings:

Household Goods: 0.98 correlation
Education: 0.98 correlation
Health: 0.97 correlation
Transport: 0.97 correlation
Food: 0.96 correlation
